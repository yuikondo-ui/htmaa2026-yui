I'm Yui Kondo, a first-year master student at Viral Communications at the MIT Media Lab. 
My research focuses on investigating the impact of algorithm-driven decision makings upon our everyday lives, with a particular emphasis on children and adolescents. My project  
[Algorithmic Mirror](projects/algorithmic-mirror.html){data-preview="img/about/algorithmic-mirror.jpg"} invites reflection on how children’s online footprints may be silently transformed into profiles, and how inhabiting such mirrors might alter one’s sense of self.

Before MIT I completed an MSc Social Science of the Internet at the Oxford Internet Institute (UK) and BA Political Science at Waseda Univeristy (Japan). 

Outside research, my high school won a national championship in  
[karuta](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sx2UqSxAPfk){data-preview="img/about/karuta.jpg"}, a Japanese competitive poetry card game.

More of my projects are on [my website](https://yuikondo.cargo.site/).
